Privacy Policy

VoiceEnhancer.io is built around browser-native audio processing. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed locally, what limited technical data may be collected, and how we protect user trust.

Last updated:

1. Introduction

VoiceEnhancer.io provides a professional voice processing workstation that runs directly inside the user’s browser. Our core product philosophy is simple: your audio should remain yours. The tool is designed for podcasters, narrators, creators, educators, remote professionals, and independent producers who want fast vocal cleanup without uploading sensitive recordings to a remote cloud service.

This Privacy Policy describes how VoiceEnhancer.io handles information when you visit the website, use the browser-based audio workstation, read educational content, or contact us by email. We use plain language wherever possible so that creators can understand the practical impact of our privacy model.

2. Local Audio Processing

The most important privacy feature of VoiceEnhancer.io is client-side processing. When you select an audio file, supported browser APIs read and process that file inside your device memory. Noise reduction, filtering, equalization, dynamic compression, peak limiting, waveform rendering, silence trimming, and export preparation are designed to happen locally in your browser session.

We do not require account creation to use the core tool, and we do not intentionally collect your raw audio recordings, transcripts, voiceprints, spoken words, private scripts, client files, podcast drafts, or exported WAV masters. Because the processing is browser-native, the performance and maximum file size can depend on your device, browser, RAM availability, and file format.

3. Information We May Collect Automatically

Like most websites, VoiceEnhancer.io may collect limited technical information automatically through server logs, browser signals, analytics tools, and advertising technologies. This may include approximate device type, browser version, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, general country or region, screen size, language preference, time spent on page, and interaction events such as whether a tool result was generated.

This information helps us understand whether the website loads correctly, which educational sections are useful, which browser versions need compatibility improvements, and whether layout changes improve accessibility. We do not use this technical information to identify the actual speaker in an audio recording.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

VoiceEnhancer.io may use cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies for essential preferences, performance measurement, ad delivery, fraud prevention, and basic site functionality. For example, local storage may remember a non-sensitive interface preference, while analytics cookies may help estimate returning visits and general usage trends.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If you block cookies, the core audio workstation should remain usable, but some preferences, analytics, or advertising-related features may not work as intended.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

To keep the tool free, VoiceEnhancer.io may display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. Advertising partners may use cookies or device identifiers to deliver, measure, and improve ads. These partners operate under their own privacy policies and may process data according to their independent compliance obligations.

We do not provide advertisers with your uploaded audio files because the workstation is designed to process audio locally. However, ad partners may receive standard web advertising signals such as page URL, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data.

6. How We Use Information

We use limited website information to operate and improve VoiceEnhancer.io, diagnose errors, monitor security, measure page performance, understand aggregate user engagement, optimize educational content, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, and support monetization that keeps the tool free. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain a reasonable support record.

We do not sell your raw audio recordings. We do not use private user recordings to train machine learning models. We do not publish user files, and we do not claim ownership over your processed audio exports.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate hosting, analytics, advertising, security, and email support. We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, valid government request, fraud investigation, protection of rights, or safety-related necessity. Any sharing is limited to what is reasonably needed for the stated purpose.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the website and related systems. These measures may include HTTPS, access controls, security monitoring, software updates, and periodic review of integrations. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but our privacy-by-design approach reduces risk by avoiding unnecessary server-side handling of user audio.

9. Data Retention

Server logs, analytics records, advertising reports, and email support messages may be retained for a reasonable period for operational, legal, accounting, and security reasons. Local audio files processed in your browser are not stored by us as part of normal tool operation. Closing the browser tab or clearing browser memory generally removes temporary local processing data.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information associated with you. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a privacy request, contact us at support@voiceenhancer.io and provide enough detail for us to verify and respond appropriately.

11. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or similar jurisdictions, our lawful bases may include legitimate interests, consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, and security-related necessity. Examples include using essential logs to secure the service, analytics to improve performance, and email content to respond to direct support requests.

12. CCPA and Similar Privacy Rights

California users may have the right to know what categories of personal information are collected, request deletion, correct inaccurate information, opt out of certain sharing, and avoid discrimination for exercising privacy rights. VoiceEnhancer.io does not sell raw user audio recordings. Advertising cookies may be treated differently under evolving privacy laws, so users should manage consent and browser settings as needed.

13. Children’s Privacy

VoiceEnhancer.io is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, they should contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

14. Third-Party Links

The website may link to guides, platforms, standards, podcast services, audio resources, or advertisers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. Always review the privacy policy of any external service before sharing information.

15. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” signals. Because there is no universal technical standard for responding to these signals, our response may vary. You can still manage cookies, ad personalization, and tracking permissions through browser tools and third-party privacy settings.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements, legal changes, new features, or operational requirements. The “Last updated” date will be revised when meaningful changes are made. Continued use of VoiceEnhancer.io after updates means you accept the updated policy.

Contact: For privacy questions or data requests, email support@voiceenhancer.io.
